## What it covers

<CardGrid stagger>
	<Card title="Service desk" icon="comment-alt">
		Tickets by number, notes, attachments and the originating email, ticket history and who changed what, routing moves in one write — and an interactive ticket card in MCP Apps hosts.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Time, billing & contracts" icon="document">
		Time entries and "log my time", billing items, contracts, services, adjustments and charges, quotes, invoices — including the contract writes the API makes awkward.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Vendor billing reconciliation" icon="random">
		Import vendor invoices (Pax8 first), find what isn't billed or is priced under cost, apply a pricing policy, and fix it with reviewed change plans.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Reports" icon="list-format">
		Unbilled work, block hours, SLA compliance, ticket throughput, project P&L, recurring revenue, time-entry compliance, inventory.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Companies & contacts" icon="setting">
		Name guards, duplicate finders, safe ID-only merges with verification, and bulk cleanup of placeholder companies and junk contacts.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Projects, CIs, service calls" icon="puzzle">
		Projects, phases, tasks and labour plans; configuration items with entitlement; dispatching, to-dos, resources and roles.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>

## Built for a live tenant

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Postgres mirror" icon="seti:db">
		About 80% of reads are answered locally with no Autotask call; a nightly check re-reads a sample and repairs differences. [How it works →](/autotask-mcp/guides/mirror/)
	</Card>
	<Card title="Change plans & bulk jobs" icon="approve-check">
		Risky changes are plans with a hash that a person approves. Mass changes run a canary first, then a paced worker. [Bulk cleanup →](/autotask-mcp/guides/bulk-cleanup/)
	</Card>
	<Card title="Throttling" icon="warning">
		Autotask allows 10,000 calls an hour for the whole tenant. The server cools down on 429s, yields background work first and shows who uses the hour. [Throttling →](/autotask-mcp/guides/throttling/)
	</Card>
	<Card title="Admin console" icon="laptop">
		Usage, callers, mirror health, feature switches without a restart, bulk jobs, and when your team works. [Console →](/autotask-mcp/guides/console/)
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
